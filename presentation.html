Solar-Aware Charging

The charger follows the sun in real time

☼
Solar panels are producing 3,200 watts
The system reads this from the inverter every 3 seconds
🔋
Battery is at 85% charge (8.5 kWh available)
Battery state is read alongside solar production
⚗
EVerest sets the charger to 13.3 amps
3,200W ÷ 240V = 13.3A — matches solar exactly
⚡
Your EV charges from sunshine, not the grid
When clouds pass, the current drops. When the sun returns, it ramps back up.
